Overview

An automation flow where the user inputs a topic and AI generates ready-to-use social media captions and hashtags in seconds.

01User inputs a topic
02AI generates platform-ready captions
03AI generates relevant hashtag sets
Challenge

Writing fresh, on-brand captions and finding the right hashtags for every post takes significant time — especially when managing content across multiple platforms or clients. The blank-page problem slows content pipelines to a crawl.

Approach

Built an AI-powered automation flow using structured prompt engineering. The user inputs a topic, and the system generates platform-ready captions and relevant hashtag sets in seconds. Prompts were designed to maintain consistent brand tone and output content that can be dropped straight into scheduling tools.

Outcome

Eliminated the blank-page problem for social media content creation. Caption and hashtag generation that used to take 15–30 minutes per post now takes seconds, freeing up time for strategy, design, and engagement.
