Challenge

A local Filipino restaurant needed an online presence that reflected their warm, homegrown brand — with no existing website, digital menu, or way for customers to find them online.

Approach

Designed and developed a full landing page using Next.js and Tailwind CSS. Focused on visual storytelling — featuring the menu, brand identity, and location in a clean, mobile-first layout. Deployed on Vercel for fast, free hosting.

Outcome

The restaurant now has a live, professional web presence that customers can find via search and social media links. The owner can share the URL directly with new customers.
